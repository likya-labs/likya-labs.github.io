Hush

A calm tap-away puzzle. Let the leaves go.

How to play

Tap a leaf whose path to the edge is clear and the current carries it away. A leaf drifts the way its tip points; anything in that line holds it back. Clear every leaf to finish the board. The first five boards of Autumn Creek teach the game step by step.

Slips and lives

Each board forgives a few taps on blocked leaves (slips). Run out and the board is lost, along with one of five lives. A life comes back every 30 minutes, or right away with an optional short video. The full version gives unlimited lives.

Purchases

Full version opens all 20 creeks, gives unlimited lives and free hints, and removes all ads. Remove ads removes the ads between boards. Both are one-time purchases. To restore them on a new device, open Settings › Restore purchase while signed in with the same Apple Account.

Today's board

One board a day, the same for everyone. It opens after your first ten creek boards.
