Privacy Policy

Hush: Leaf Puzzle · last updated 16 November 2026

What Hush itself collects

Nothing. Hush has no accounts and no analytics of its own, and we do not run any servers that receive data from the app.

What stays on your device

Your progress (cleared boards, daily streak, lives) and your settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: an ad between some boards, and optional reward videos you choose to watch for a life, a hint or extra slips. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, your IP address (from which an approximate location can be derived), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google uses this as described in its policy on partner apps.

Hush asks for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before the advertising identifier can be used; if you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalised. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choices first, and you can change them any time under Settings › Ad privacy choices.

Buying the full version removes all ads. "Remove ads" removes the ads between boards; reward videos stay optional.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We never receive your payment details.

Children

Hush is a general-audience game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children; ads are served by Google under its own policies.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. Questions: likyalabs@gmail.com. Support: Hush support.
